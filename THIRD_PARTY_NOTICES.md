## Algorithms independently implemented in `hmvc.features`

`src/hmvc/features` contains **independent NumPy implementations** of published
signal-processing algorithms. No source code from the projects below was copied
or translated, and none of them is a runtime or test dependency:

- CheapTrick spectral envelope: M. Morise, "CheapTrick, a spectral envelope
  estimator for high-quality speech synthesis", Speech Communication 67, 2015.
  Its conventions (window, q1 lifter, FFT-size rule) follow the WORLD vocoder
  (https://github.com/mmorise/World, BSD-3-Clause) so results can be compared.
- Mel-cepstral analysis conventions (all-pass `freqt`, `mcepalpha`, sp2mc/mc2sp
  scaling) follow SPTK (https://github.com/sp-nitech/SPTK) and pysptk, for
  interoperability.
- YIN: A. de Cheveigné and H. Kawahara, JASA 111(4), 2002. Band aperiodicity
  uses the harmonicity estimate of P. Boersma, IFA Proceedings 17, 1993.

`tests/data/feature-reference/` stores numeric **outputs** of pyworld, pysptk
and librosa, computed on HMVC's own synthetic test signals. These are test-only
oracles generated by `tools/generate_feature_reference.py` and are not packaged.
